📋 Crawler Methodology & Standards
01
Source of Recruitment Data

All active notices and vacancy counts are crawled verbatim from the official state portals, including **WBPSC**, **WBPRB**, and **WBHEALTH**. 📄 View Persistent JSON Database →

02
Crawl & Verification Hierarchy

We follow a strict verification protocol: (1) Direct machine-parsed HTML tables and official PDF notices from the state domain — the gold standard. (2) Verified secondary announcement channels. We do not index job openings from third-party private aggregation sites, social media groups, or unverified leaks.

03
How a Notice is Inferred & Updated

Notices are parsed in real-time. A notice status is marked as 'open' (Apply Online) when the application window is active. It automatically shifts to 'closed' once the parsed closing deadline has elapsed. Critical updates (admit cards, exam dates) are dynamically appended to the milestones timeline.
